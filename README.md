## Overview

NyayaSaathi is an AI-powered legal assistant designed to make Indian law understandable, accessible, and actionable for women across India.

Women often face legal issues without knowing their rights, the relevant laws, or where to seek help. NyayaSaathi bridges this gap by offering instant, plain-language legal guidance.

A user simply describes her situation in natural language. NyayaSaathi then:

- Identifies the relevant legal domain
- Explains her rights in simple language
- Provides a personalized step-by-step action plan
- Connects her to verified legal aid organizations
- Offers continued support through follow-up questions

No legal jargon. No consultation fees. No barriers.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of women in India face legal challenges but remain unaware of their rights.

- 70% of domestic violence survivors never report abuse
- One in three working women faces workplace harassment
- 85% are unaware of free legal aid services
- Legal consultations are often financially inaccessible

Common barriers include:

- Lack of awareness
- Complex legal terminology
- Uncertainty about next steps
- Fear of the legal system
- Limited access to affordable assistance

NyayaSaathi eliminates these barriers through accessible, AI-driven legal support.

## How It Works

1. The user describes her legal situation in natural language.
2. Claude identifies the relevant legal domain and applicable laws.
3. NyayaSaathi explains her rights in clear, simple terms.
4. A personalized step-by-step legal roadmap is generated.
5. Verified legal aid organizations and helplines are recommended.
6. The user can continue asking follow-up questions.

---

## Example

User Input:

> My husband has stopped giving me money and doesn't allow me to speak to my family.

NyayaSaathi Response:

- Identifies the issue as domestic violence
- Explains rights under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
- Details rights to maintenance, residence, and protection
- Provides immediate next steps
- Connects the user to helplines and nearby support services
